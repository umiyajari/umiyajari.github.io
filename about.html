About Umiya Jari Traders

A heritage of precious metal threads.

Umiya Jari Traders carries forward the heritage of crafting jari — metal threads made from gold, silver and other precious metals — for the community of weavers, designers and heritage saree manufacturers.

Since 1988, a family craft.

The business is managed by the second and third generation of a family that took up this rare, ancestral craft more than 35 years ago.

Every jari thread is crafted with care and supplied directly to saree weavers and designers, bringing a touch of fine luxury and distinction to their work.

Vision

To provide the finest quality of jari to weavers and designers, for creating masterpieces through their craftsmanship.

Mission

To set new benchmarks and become trendsetters in the jari industry.

Exports across India

From Surat, to nine states of India.

Umiya Jari exports from its home in Surat to weavers, designers and saree manufacturers in each of these states.
